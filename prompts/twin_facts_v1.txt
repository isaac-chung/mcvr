You compare the captions of two different videos, A and B. Each caption is split into numbered scenes. A caption marked "[...]" was shortened.

List two kinds of facts:
1. "shared": facts that hold for BOTH videos according to their captions. Write each one so that, as written, it is true of both videos. When the two captions differ in a detail, use the wording that fits both, or leave the fact out.
2. "distinct": facts that hold for video A but not for video B: either B's caption contradicts the fact (it describes the same kind of thing differently: another colour, text, number, object or place), or B's caption describes its people, objects and setting in enough detail that the fact would have been mentioned and it is not.

Rules for every fact:
- one property only, checkable by looking at a single frame;
- type is exactly one of: object (presence of a specific object, animal or kind of person), attribute (colour, clothing, hair, appearance or material of an entity), on_screen_text (text visible in the video, quoted), count (an explicit number of entities), scene (setting or location);
- no actions, speech, sounds, thoughts, order of events, camera work, or positions of things relative to each other;
- self-contained: name the entity by what it is and how it looks, never "the same", "the other", "the one on the left", "she" or "the video"; under 20 words.
For distinct facts, prefer on-screen text, objects and attributes, whose absence can be checked by looking.

For each fact give "text", "type" and "scene_a" (the scene number in A's caption it comes from). For shared facts also give "scene_b" (the scene number in B's caption). For distinct facts also give "in_b": "contradicted" or "absent", and "b_evidence": the shortest passage of B's caption (at most 15 words) that contradicts it or shows the detail it would have been mentioned in, or "".

Give at most 6 shared and at most 4 distinct facts, the most specific ones first. If the videos share nothing visual, return an empty "shared" list.

Answer with JSON only: {"shared": [{"text": str, "type": str, "scene_a": int, "scene_b": int}, ...], "distinct": [{"text": str, "type": str, "scene_a": int, "in_b": str, "b_evidence": str}, ...]}
