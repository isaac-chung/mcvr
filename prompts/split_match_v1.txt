A search query for a video was written from a list of facts about the video (the gold facts). An automatic splitter then split the query into constraints. Compare the two lists.

For each gold fact, say how the split constraints cover it:
- "full": one constraint, or several together, state the whole fact (wording may differ; exact text, numbers and colours must agree).
- "partial": the constraints state only part of the fact, or change a detail.
- "none": no constraint states it.
For each split constraint, give the number of the gold fact it states (or part of), or null when it states none of them (generic, added, temporal-only or about something else).

Gold facts:
{facts}

Split constraints:
{constraints}

Answer with JSON only: {"facts": [{"fact": 1, "match": "full" or "partial" or "none", "constraints": [constraint numbers]}, ...], "constraints": [{"constraint": 1, "fact": fact number or null}, ...]}
